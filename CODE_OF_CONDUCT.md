# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to keep the project practical, respectful, and focused on reliable
runtime governance for AI agent tooling.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical disagreements with concrete evidence.
- Keep issues and pull requests scoped to the stated problem.
- Respect security reports and avoid public disclosure of unpatched
  vulnerabilities.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, or disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block participants who
make collaboration unsafe or unproductive. Security-sensitive reports should use
the process in `SECURITY.md`.
